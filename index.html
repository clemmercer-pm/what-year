WHAT YEAR??

Three things. One year. Name it.

    How to play

    • You get three clues - each a different thing that happened in the same year.
    • Guess the exact year, using the BC / AD toggle.
    • You have 6 guesses. A new clue unlocks on your 3rd and 5th guess.
    • Each guess tells you too early ↑ or too late ↓, and if you’re in the right decade.
    • Then share your result - no spoilers. A new puzzle every day.
